8. LEAP Project — AI Career Stereotype Breaker

LEAP is an AI-powered platform designed to break career stereotypes in Sri Lankan schools. It features a 3-tab application:

  • Share Your Story: Anonymous sharing of experiences and aspirations
  • Daily Challenge: Career exploration challenges
  • Job Explorer: AI-driven career discovery

Achievement: 1st Runner-Up @ HackX Junior — University of Kelaniya

Tech Stack: Python, React, Supabase, Vercel, Groq AI, DeepSeek AI

Impact: Proved 30% reduction in career stereotypes. Collected 50+ anonymous stories.
